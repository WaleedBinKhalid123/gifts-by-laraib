### Notes for when we use these

- **Nested baskets are useful.** #15 and #16 each show three sizes in one shot,
  which maps straight onto the Mini / Classic / Deluxe variants.
- **Shot on a shaggy rug in low light.** Fine for reference and for the Customize
  item thumbnails, but the hero and shop-card photos want a plain, brighter
  surface — a white sheet or cream card near a window. Worth reshooting the
  finished baskets rather than the components.
- **#14 florist tape** is a workshop supply, not something a customer picks. It
  probably shouldn't appear on the site at all.
