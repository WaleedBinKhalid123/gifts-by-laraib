The Glow Hour

Everything for the hour before she walks out the door.

A beauty-led basket built entirely from stock already on the shelf — the square wicker trays, the hot pink roses, the Emelie and Meidián lines, one plush bear. Nothing here needs buying in. The two self-care baskets on the site are wind-down baskets; this one is the opposite, and that gap is why it's next.

Colour rule

The packaging is already loud — purple compacts, lime soap tubes, magenta gloss boxes. So the basket stays to five colours and the products supply the noise. If a product's box clashes badly, it faces backwards or sits under something. Nothing gets added just to fill a gap.

03 · Build plan

Seven steps, back to front

Classic size shown
  1. 1

    Liner

    Crush blush tissue into the tray so it mounds slightly at the centre and the corners stand just above the rim. It hides the weave gaps and stops small tubes sinking. Never lay it flat.

  2. 2

    Bear, back-left

    The pink hooded bear seated in the back-left corner, turned about 45° toward the camera so the cream face reads. On Mini and Classic this is a keychain bear instead — same corner.

  3. 3

    Rose posy, back-right

    Cut seven hot pink stems to roughly 4 inches, tape them into a single posy with two gypsophila sprays, then wedge it into the corner so the heads sit an inch above the rim. One posy, not seven separate stems — separate stems drift in transit.

  4. 4

    Flat-lay band, middle

    Blush palette laid flat, lid closed, logo facing forward. Sheet mask sachets fanned behind it like a hand of cards so all three names are readable. This band is what the overhead photo is for.

  5. 5

    Front row

    Paper soap tubes standing at each end as bookends. Lip glosses upright in a row between them. Hand cream tubes laid horizontally along the very front edge, caps all facing the same way. Nothing in this row taller than the palette behind it.

  6. 6

    Keychain corner

    Front-right stays deliberately low: the mini compact face-up, and the keychain bear clipped to the rim so it hangs slightly outside the basket. That overhang is what makes the shot look handmade rather than packed.

  7. 7

    Ribbon and card

    Hot pink satin around the basket body, bow tied at front-left, tails cut on the diagonal. Handwritten card tucked between the palette and the roses, writing side out. One glitter jar beside the bow on Deluxe only.

The one rule that matters

Every item has to be liftable without moving another one. If a customer has to dig, the basket was over-packed — and an over-packed basket photographs as a pile.

Please, not the rug

Every reference photo above was shot on the black-and-grey shaggy rug in low light. It's fine for stock records and it's what the Customize thumbnails can keep using. But the shop cards need a plain bright ground — a white sheet or cream card near a window, mid-morning, no flash. Same basket, twice the price on the page.
