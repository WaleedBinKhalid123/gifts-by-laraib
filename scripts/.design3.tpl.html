01 · The format

Flowers frame it. Gifts fill it.

Not a bouquet box
Why this sells better than a full flower box

A box packed edge-to-edge with roses costs you 30+ stems and gives the customer nothing to keep. This uses 12 and puts a perfume in the middle. Cheaper to make, more to open, and the photograph is better because there is something to look at in the centre.

04 · Build order

Five moves

Ring first, gifts last
1Foam ring, hollow centre
2Roses in, evenly spaced
3Gypsophila in the gaps
4Perfume centre, rest around it
5Lid on, ribbon, bow
The one thing to get right

Cut every rose to the same 3 inches and push them to the same depth. An uneven ring is the single thing that makes a flower box look homemade, and it is the only part of this design that is hard to fix once the foam is glued.
